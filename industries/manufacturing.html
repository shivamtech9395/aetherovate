4 Specialized Modules Built For Your Factory Floor

1. Raw Material Batch & Inventory Tracker

Every lot of steel, plastic, chemical, or fabric gets a QR code. System maps which raw material lot went into which finished product batch.

2. Quality Inspection & Rejection Log

Quality controllers fill digital checklists on tablet at assembly line. Rejected parts auto-log cause (defect, dimension, scratch) with photo upload.

3. Worker Shift & Overtime Payroll Tool

Biometric face/finger punch logs exact check-in. Auto-computes double overtime, late deductions, and prints monthly salary slips in 1-click.

4. Machine Preventive Maintenance Alert

Schedule oil changes, motor servicing, and calibration by running hours. System alerts plant engineer before breakdown stops the line.
